# v0.23 design seed — Opinion Artifact

This experiment translates a user-provided AXM visual-campaign concept into a deterministic contract. The image itself is not imported into WALDO and is not treated as runtime evidence.

The preserved design ideas are:

- do not honestly claim consciousness without evidence;
- allow a future machine identity to form and state an opinion;
- require the opinion to show how it was reached;
- retain source packets and competing evidence;
- retain dissent instead of smoothing it away;
- make uncertainty visible;
- keep the artifact traceable, reversible, and open to review;
- keep opinion separate from authority, execution, promotion, and CANON.

The poster phrase `HUMAN AUTHORITY — FINAL DECISION` is translated more narrowly in code as `authority: NONE` for the artifact itself. The contract does not attempt to define every future AXM governance actor; it only proves that the opinion artifact cannot grant authority to itself.

The visual was a conceptual seed, not evidence that a live machine opinion or conscious state already exists.
